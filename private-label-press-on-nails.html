Private-label handmade press-on nails from Yunwan

Yunwan is a handmade press-on nail team and factory in Xiaogan, Hubei, China. We support small trial orders, dropshipping, custom designs, custom sizing and private-label OEM.

Start with a trial order

A trial order can help a buyer check style direction, sizing communication, workmanship and delivery coordination before deciding which designs to reorder. Yunwan supports small trial orders; an exact wholesale minimum is confirmed for each order rather than published as a universal figure.

Customization options

Available cooperation includes customization from reference images, style customization, size customization and private-label manufacturing. Feasibility, quantity and timing should be confirmed for each design.

Lead time

Ready-stock items and ordinary custom orders are usually dispatched within 24 to 48 hours, depending on quantity. Complex designs, larger batches and brand projects require a separate schedule.

Overseas delivery support

Yunwan can coordinate in English, support international logistics, cross-border payment and customs assistance, or deliver to a buyer's freight-forwarder warehouse in China. Country coverage, payment method and transit time are confirmed per order.
