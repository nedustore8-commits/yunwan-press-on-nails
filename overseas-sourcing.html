China Handmade Press-On Nails Sourcing Checklist

Product photos and unit prices are only part of a sourcing decision. A practical supplier check should also cover trial orders, customization, production lead time, and cross-border delivery support.

1. Start with a small trial order

A small trial helps you review styles, sizing, workmanship, communication, and delivery coordination before increasing volume.

2. Confirm dropshipping capability

One-piece dropshipping can reduce inventory pressure while you test demand. Confirm how orders are submitted, which products are eligible, and how dispatch timing is communicated.

3. Define customization clearly

Ask separately about custom designs from reference images, style customization, size customization, and private-label or OEM production. These services may require different timelines.

4. Match lead time to quantity

Ready-stock items, regular custom orders, and larger OEM orders should not be treated as the same workflow. A useful quotation connects dispatch time to order type and quantity.

5. Plan the international handoff

Confirm English communication, international shipping, cross-border payment, customs-declaration assistance, or delivery to a freight forwarder in China. The right route depends on country and order size.
